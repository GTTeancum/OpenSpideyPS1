MAKE YOUR OWN SPIDER-MAN 2 RESKIN

Copy this entire folder into mods/suits beside SpiderMan2.exe. Give your copy
a new folder name, such as my-spidey. Six built-in bases are available: default
SM1 Spider-Man, Scarlet Spider, Symbiote Spider-Man, Quick Change, Peter Parker,
and default SM2 Spider-Man with wings.
No external model or disc is needed for the costume.

1. Paint your costume

Open the PNG pictures in textures in your image editor. Recolor or paint them,
then save with the same names. Keep body parts and transparent areas in place.
Larger pictures, including 2048 x 2048, work; keep the original proportions.
The supplied enlarged picture tests this support; enlarging alone adds no detail.

TEMPLATE contains painting guides made with Blender's UV Layout exporter.
White outlines show the model faces. TEMPLATE/UV has separate outline layers.
Paint the clean pictures in textures and leave guide lines out of the result.
The game ignores TEMPLATE. SM1 and SM2 have different layouts: use this SM2
template for the sm2-spiderman body. SM1 pictures also work in SM2 when
you keep their matching SM1 model base; see README.md for migration details.

2. Name it and choose its powers

Open suit.json in Notepad and change the text inside quotation marks:

  id        A unique label: lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens; no spaces.
  name      The costume name in both panels (18 characters maximum).
  comments  Your message under COMMENTS:. Keep it to three short lines.
  model     The built-in model base. This example uses sm2-spiderman.
  profile   A power choice below; it does not change your painted model.

Leave version and textures alone. They match the sm2-spiderman base. Other model
bases require their matching texture/template set. Keep the commas and quotation
marks. You do not need an external model or another JSON file.

Model choices:

  spiderman         Default SM1 Dreamcast Spider-Man.
  scarlet-spider    Dreamcast Scarlet Spider.
  symbiote          Dreamcast Symbiote Spider-Man.
  quick-change      Dreamcast Quick Change Spider-Man.
  peter-parker      Dreamcast Peter Parker.
  sm2-spiderman     Default SM2 Dreamcast Spider-Man, including web wings.

Your painted appearance and powers donor are independent. For example, keep
model "sm2-spiderman" and set profile "prodigy" for double jump, stronger attacks
and better web swinging. "alex-ross-red" gives double jump alone; "alex-ross-white"
gives better web swinging alone. "symbiote" gives unlimited webbing.
"unlimited" is the Unlimited costume's STEALTH profile, not unlimited webbing.

Choose ONE profile. You cannot mix donors or add enemy movesets with JSON.
The original donor need not be unlocked. Changing powers leaves your PNGs alone.
All 19 choices and a JSON example are in README.md.

Power choices (the same powers as the corresponding original SM2 costume):

  spiderman         Normal Spider-Man.
  spider-phoenix    Invulnerability, stronger attacks and better web swinging.
  prodigy           Double jump, stronger attacks and better web swinging.
  dusk              Stealth.
  insulated         Stronger attacks and electrical resistance.
  alex-ross-red     Double jump.
  alex-ross-white   Better web swinging.
  venom-earth-x    Unlimited webbing and stronger attacks.
  negative-zone    Normal Spider-Man.
  symbiote         Unlimited webbing.
  2099             Stronger attacks.
  captain-universe Invulnerability, stronger attacks and unlimited webbing.
  unlimited        Stealth.
  bagman           No web belt.
  scarlet          Normal Spider-Man.
  ben-reilly       Normal Spider-Man.
  quick-change     No web belt.
  peter-parker     No web belt.
  battle-damaged   Normal Spider-Man.

GAME POWERS: is filled in automatically using the original costume wording.

3. Try it and share it

Restart the game and open SPECIAL, then COSTUMES. Select your costume;
reskins are always unlocked. Up to 12 reskins can be installed at once,
including this example, alongside the 19 original costumes. If a donor with more
power lines makes your mod fail validation, shorten comments to fit the panel.

If yours is missing, check its unique id, PNG files, commas and quotation
marks. Shorten long comments. To share it, zip your folder with suit.json,
instructions.txt and textures. Other players put it in their mods/suits.
